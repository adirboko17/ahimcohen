Respiratory Protection Products

Goma respiratory protection products are divided into power air supply filter respirator, positive pressure air respirator, electroacoustic gas mask, self-priming filter gas mask, self-priming filter filter, self-priming filter anti particle respirator, self-priming filter escape respirator, etc., which can be used for eye and respiratory protection of operators in chemical, construction, mining, welding, paint spraying, agriculture, medicine and other operations, It can also be applied to respiratory protection for operators in non war military operations such as nuclear, chemical, and biological emergency rescue, secondary nuclear, chemical, and biological disasters.

Head Protection Products

Gaoma head protection products mainly include various safety helmet products. The Gaoma safety helmet series provides users with a variety of material selection needs, and the cap shell is made of high impact ABS engineering materials, polypropylene (PP) materials, and fiber reinforced composite (FRP) materials specifically designed for safety helmets. The Gaoma safety helmet series products not only meet the various safety performance indicators of industrial safety helmets, but also have fashionable hat shell design and wide application fields; At the same time, there are simple slots on both sides, which can be equipped with high Ma protective masks or other soundproofing devices. The product lining adopts a breathable design with sweat absorption bands, greatly improving the user's breathability and comfort when wearing.
