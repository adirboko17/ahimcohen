Introducing Tiger Grip

Thanks to Tiger Grip, safety overshoes can now be as comfortable, lightweight and secure as they are convenient. Providing the perfect and most cost effective solution for temporary workers, visitors, service engineers and anyone needing occasional access to designated safety footwear zones.
