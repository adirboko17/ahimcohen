We are Top Glove Europe

Top Glove Corporation BHD is the world’s largest manufacturer of gloves and it is our mission to supply our customers all over the world with consistent high-quality gloves. We currently produce 100 Billion gloves per year in our 50 factories located in Malaysia, Thailand, China and Vietnam.

As European representative, we are responsible for sales inquiries from all European countries and business partnerships, as well as all legal inquiries regarding the European standards and regulations.

Our products

We provide quality. For every standard.

Whether for medical sector, industrial use or for consumers: We offer our gloves in a wide range of qualities, materials, thicknesses, sizes and colors. Whether Nitrile, Latex, Vinyl or TPE - we can match even individual requirements.

For medical sector, we produce a large number of gloves not only fit for examination purposes and usage in home cares, but also higher quality surgical gloves worn by doctors and surgeons.

In industrial sector, we have a range of heavy-duty products with a higher weight and length to make them more durable and chemical resistant for a harsher work environment. Furthermore, we produce a number of special products including children’s gloves and our Biogreen biodegradable gloves.
