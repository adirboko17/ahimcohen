About Allen Cooper

Allen Cooper is a flagship brand of Superhouse Group.In addition to manufacturing of PPE; Allen Cooper has a vast legacy of manufacturing and export of quality leather footwear, Sports shoes, fashion utility products and accessories. Allen Cooper is one of the fastest growing Retail chain in North India and has emerged as one of the most preferred brand to the customers for exclusive leather & sports footwear, apparels and accessories. The products of Allen Cooper are now available across multiple windows.

GLOBAL FOOTPRINT

Superhouse Group : proud owner of its global brand “ALLEN COOPER” is one of the leading manufacturers of leather, leather products and textile garments across the globe. From a single tannery in early 80’s producing finished leather, Superhouse is now an over US$ 100 million company. Superhouse Group has grown from strength to strength in last 4 decades of its existence and has emerged as one of the biggest players in the field of leather, textile and footwear industry. Group is a true Indian multinational and operating through its various manufacturing units located in India and Abroad.
