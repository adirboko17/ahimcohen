Emergency medical service (EMS) provides care in a variety of settings, from calm to chaotic. First responders often face challenges such as extricating patients from a motor vehicle crash, where people become trapped in mangled vehicles. The essential focus for EMS providers, regardless of the situation or scene, is personal safety.

In any accident scene, the rapid removal of the injured to a hospital improves their chances of survival greatly. An EMS provider is of no use to a patient if he or she is injured or incapacitated. More importantly, Emergency medical services can be a dangerous job and responders must ensure that they go home safe at the end of every shift. Having the proper safety gear is paramount.

SPECIALIZED HAND PROTECTION

Specialized hand protection is very important when dealing with vehicle extrication. A first responder’s hands are typically exposed to hazards such as glass, glass dust, shards and splinters of metal, plastic and other sharp materials all of which can destroy flesh in seconds.
