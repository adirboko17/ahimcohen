About us

At Alexandra, we have proudly supported the UK’s healthcare professionals for over a century. Every uniform we make is designed to help those who dedicate their lives to caring for others, from nurses on night shifts to hospital staff working behind the scenes.

This Christmas, we are donating 10% of every sale from our Christmas Collection to Great Ormond Street Hospital. It is our way of giving back to the community that inspires us every day, and supporting a hospital that represents hope, compassion and the best of British healthcare.
